About Kisiki Translator

Kisiki is a free online English to Kiswahili translator. Simply type any English word or sentence and get an instant Kiswahili translation. No download required.

Who is this for?

This tool is perfect for students, teachers, travellers, and anyone learning Kiswahili across East Africa including Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania, and Rwanda.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Kisiki is a free translation tool. We believe in being transparent about how the app works and what (very little) data is involved.

1. Who We Are

Kisiki Translator is a free online tool that helps users translate between English and Kiswahili. It is operated as a personal project and is not affiliated with any company.

For questions, contact us at: smartfuture437@gmail.com

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Kisiki does not collect, store, or share your personal information. Specifically, we do not:

  • Require you to create an account or log in
  • Store the text you enter for translation
  • Collect your name, email address, or location
  • Sell or share any data with third parties

3. Third-Party Translation API

Kisiki uses the MyMemory API to process translations. When you click "Translate", your input text is sent to MyMemory's servers to retrieve the translation result.

MyMemory may collect usage data in accordance with their own privacy policy. We encourage you to review it at mymemory.translated.net.

We recommend you avoid entering sensitive or personal information in the translation box.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

Kisiki may use your browser's local storage to save translation history for your convenience. This data:

  • Is stored only on your own device
  • Is never sent to our servers
  • Can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser's site data

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

5. Analytics

We may use basic analytics tools (such as those provided by Netlify or Google Analytics) to understand how many people visit the site and which features are used. This data is anonymous and used only to improve the app.

6. Children's Privacy

Kisiki is suitable for users of all ages, including students and learners. We do not knowingly collect any personal data from children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.
